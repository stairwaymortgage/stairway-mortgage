## Why Your Income Needs Careful Documentation

Federal air traffic controllers employed through the FAA receive compensation that is layered by design. Your earnings likely include:

- **Base pay** tied to your pay band and step
- **Locality pay** that adjusts for the regional cost of living
- **Shift differentials** for overnight and weekend coverage
- **Overtime**, which can be substantial during periods of understaffing

Recent labor developments in the ATC field — including pay adjustments negotiated between the FAA and the controllers' union — mean that some controllers have seen income changes within the last year or two. When your pay has shifted recently, lenders need to determine what portion of your income is stable and likely to continue.

The standard approach is a two-year history documented through W-2s and federal pay stubs. If your overtime and differential pay appears consistently over that window, it may count toward your qualifying income. If it is newer, a lender might average it differently or require a written verification of employment from your facility.

## Loan Programs Worth Knowing About

Depending on your service history and the property you choose in Baker County, several loan paths may be available to you.

**VA Loans** — If you have qualifying military service in addition to your federal civilian career, a VA loan could be one of the most useful tools available, including no down payment and no private mortgage insurance requirement for eligible borrowers.

**USDA Rural Development Loans** — Given Baker County's rural character, this program deserves a close look. Properties in and around Macclenny may qualify. Income limits apply at the household level, but many ATC professionals in this region fall within those thresholds.

**Conventional Loans** — With a documented two-year earnings history, conventional financing could work well, especially if your credit profile is solid and your down payment covers at least a standard threshold.

**FHA Loans** — These tend to be more flexible on credit scores and debt ratios, which can help when your income documentation is more complex than a simple W-2 salary.

## Preparing Your File Before You Start Shopping

The single most useful thing you can do before you begin looking at homes in Baker County is to get your documentation in order. For federal ATC employees, that typically means:

- Two years of W-2s from the federal government
- Recent federal pay stubs showing your current gross pay breakdown
- A leave and earnings statement that reflects your current pay band and step
- Documentation of any secondary income, side work, or rental income
- Bank statements for assets and reserves

If you have experienced a pay adjustment — a step increase, a new locality pay tier, or a renegotiated base rate — having a current letter from your facility confirming continued employment and pay structure can smooth the underwriting process significantly.

Being organized at the start often shortens the time between pre-approval and closing, which matters in a market where sellers may be considering multiple offers.
