## How Lenders Actually Read RSU Income

Restricted stock units are compensation, but they don't look like a paycheck. Each grant has a vesting schedule. Each vested tranche may be immediately sold or held. The tax treatment, the timing, and the market value at the moment of vesting can all vary year to year — which makes RSU income one of the more nuanced line items an underwriter will review.

In many cases, a lender will want to see at least two years of RSU income that has actually vested and been received. They'll typically average that income across the vesting history. If your grants have been growing — which is common at companies where leadership compensation packages are scaling alongside performance — the average may understate what you're currently earning. That's worth understanding before you apply.

High-profile executive compensation stories have been in the news recently, from massive tech sector paydays to equity grants at semiconductor companies setting new benchmarks. That trend reflects a broader shift: a larger share of executive pay is arriving as equity rather than cash. Mortgage guidelines are catching up, but they haven't always kept pace with how compensation is actually structured at the companies where many Alachua County executives work.

## The Documentation Alachua County Executives Should Gather Early

If you know a purchase is on your horizon, start pulling these together now:

**Two years of W-2s.** Your RSU income that was liquidated and taxed will appear here. Underwriters look for consistency and an upward trend.

**Recent pay stubs.** If your employer reports vested RSU income on pay stubs, those will help show current-year earnings.

**Equity grant agreements.** These documents show the vesting schedule and confirm that future grants are part of your employment arrangement — which can support the argument that the income is likely to continue.

**Brokerage account statements.** If you've sold shares upon vesting, those transactions provide a paper trail that confirms actual dollars received.

**Your most recent two years of federal tax returns.** Underwriters will cross-reference what's reported on your 1040 with what appears on your W-2s.

Some executives in Alachua County also carry income from other sources — consulting arrangements, board compensation, or investment income. Each stream may be treated differently, and layering them together requires a loan originator who understands complex income structures.

## Why the Timing of Your Vesting Schedule Matters

If you have a large tranche of RSUs vesting soon, that timing can affect your strategy in a few ways.

First, vested shares that have been sold and sitting in a brokerage account may count as verified assets, which can strengthen your overall financial picture for underwriting purposes.

Second, if you're planning to use liquidated RSUs as part of your down payment — say, 20% of a purchase — the source of funds will need to be documented. Your lender will want to trace those dollars from the vesting event through the sale and into your account.

Third, if you're mid-cycle on a grant and a significant portion of your income story is still in unvested shares, your qualifying income may look lower than your actual earning trajectory suggests. That's a conversation worth having before you go under contract on a home in Gainesville or anywhere in Alachua County.
