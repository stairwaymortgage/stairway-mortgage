You spend your shifts managing aircraft movements over some of the busiest airspace in the country, translating split-second decisions into safe outcomes. When you are ready to buy a home in Alachua County, you might expect the mortgage process to run just as smoothly. Instead, you run into a lender who is puzzled by your pay stubs.

For a loan officer unfamiliar with federal pay structures, that complexity can slow things down or raise unnecessary red flags. ## Getting to Know the Alachua County Housing Market

Alachua County sits in north-central Florida, anchored by Gainesville, the county seat and home of the University of Florida. The market here has a character distinct from South Florida — you will find a mix of urban neighborhoods near the university, established suburban subdivisions, and genuinely rural stretches that begin just outside city limits.

That rural character matters for mortgage planning. ## How Lenders Read Your FAA Pay

The most common friction point for air traffic controllers in the mortgage process is income documentation. Here is how it typically plays out.

**Base salary and locality pay** are the most straightforward components. They appear consistently and are easy for underwriters to verify through pay stubs and W-2s.

**Overtime and shift differential** require more work. Most lenders want to see a two-year average before they will count these figures toward your qualifying income. If you have worked consistent extra shifts or overnight rotations, that history is valuable — but it needs to be documented clearly.

**Aviation Career Incentive Pay (ACIP)** and any retirement-adjacent benefits may be reviewed on a case-by-case basis. The key question underwriters ask is whether the income is likely to continue. Federal employment generally supports that argument, but having organized records makes the process smoother.

## Loan Options Worth Exploring for Controllers in Alachua County

Depending on your service history and financial profile, several loan types may be relevant.

**VA loans** are available to controllers who also served in the military or qualify through a veteran family member. This program often allows financing without a down payment and without private mortgage insurance — a meaningful advantage in any market.

**Conventional loans** remain a common path for federal employees with strong credit and documented income. Alachua County's property values in many neighborhoods fall within standard conforming loan limits, which keeps these loans accessible.

**USDA loans** may be an option for properties in eligible rural areas of the county. If the home you are considering is outside the Gainesville city limits, it is worth a quick eligibility check.

**FHA loans** offer flexibility on credit history and down payment for borrowers who may be earlier in their savings journey.
